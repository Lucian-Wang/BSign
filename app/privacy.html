隐私说明 / Privacy

PDF、图片文档与 PDF 合成在浏览器本地处理。保存到印章库的印章、签名及托管证书会加密存储在本部署服务器。服务器运营者控制密钥，这不是端到端加密。

PDF and image documents are composed locally in your browser. Saved seals, signatures and managed certificates are encrypted on this deployment's server. Operators control the keys; this is not end-to-end encryption.

Word 文件仅在启用独立转换服务后临时上传转换。邮箱验证码通过部署者配置的邮件服务发送。手机签名临时传送至服务器，过期清理。

Word documents are temporarily uploaded only when the separate conversion service is enabled. Email codes use the configured email provider. Phone handwriting is temporarily transferred through this server and expires automatically.

私有 CA 与自签证书仅用于完整性检查，不证明真实身份。本部署者须根据实际运营方式补充适用的隐私政策、联系方式与使用条款。

Private CA and self-signed certificates support integrity checks, not identity verification. The operator must provide applicable policies, contact information and terms for their deployment.
